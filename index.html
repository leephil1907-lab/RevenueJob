Questions

Straight answers.

No. A wrapper sends templated email from a shared inbox. RevenuePilot runs a persistent revenue system: prospecting agents watch intent signals, research agents build account context, conversation agents negotiate timing, and attribution agents trace the outcome to closed-won. The agent is one node — the loop is the product.

Every action is logged with the reasoning that produced it. Set autonomy to Review and the agent drafts instead of sends — you approve in bulk from the console. Most teams run review mode for two weeks, then move to autonomous with QA sampling on accounts above a spend threshold.

Native sync with Salesforce, HubSpot, Outreach, Salesloft, Snowflake, Slack and Google Workspace. Agents write structured records — not notes — so your reporting stays clean. A typed API and webhooks cover everything else, and SOC 2 Type II plus per-tenant encryption are standard on every plan.

Agents start researching within the first hour. First qualified conversations land in days 3–7, first booked meetings typically by day 11. Median time from signal to meeting across all accounts is 11 days; the fastest published rollout booked in 36 hours.

Yes — the Agent Builder lets you assemble specialists: an enterprise prospector, a PLG motion agent, a renewal agent, a partner agent. Each has its own personality dial, autonomy level, goal target and territory rules, and they share learned context so a prospect that leaves one motion enters the next already understood.

A Revenue team deployment is $499/month versus roughly $8,400/month fully loaded for a mid-market SDR — about 6% of the cost, with the coverage of a pod. On the Enterprise tier customers run 20+ agents against multiple regions for less than a single headcount.
